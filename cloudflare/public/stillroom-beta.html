Stillroom beta guide

Try Stillroom on a real image handoff

Thanks for helping us test an early Mac app. We are checking whether Stillroom makes your existing work easier. Honest problems and reasons you would not use it are useful.

Before starting

Use a Mac running macOS 14 or later. Install only through the TestFlight invitation supplied by the study organizer. This beta has not been validated on every supported Mac. Keep a backup of valuable work and use copies of a recent, non-confidential job for your first session. You never need to send your images to us.

Stillroom creates new files and leaves originals unchanged. The queue and crop positions last for the current session. Recipes, delivery sets and export history are saved locally. History contains destination folders.

Three things to try

  1. A small website handoff. Bring three images from your own work. Prepare JPEGs at the longest edge your brief requires. Compare the encoded result, export into a new folder, and open the files in your normal image viewer. Tell us anything that needed explanation.
  2. A complete delivery (Pro test access required). Prepare 10–30 images for hero, editorial and thumbnail placements. Use Delivery sets → Website launch, inspect each variant, and position any crop that cuts off an important subject. Export and inspect the folders, contact sheet and manifest. Check dimensions and file-size limits against the brief. Crops share one normalized position per source; inspect all variants.
  3. Come back for another job. During the following week, use Stillroom only if a real job makes it useful. Tell us why you returned or what you chose instead. There is no obligation to use it or buy anything.

In TestFlight, subscription purchases are sandbox tests and do not charge you. They do not carry over to an App Store release. If prices do not load or Pro does not unlock, report that; do not purchase a different product to work around it. The organizer must configure Apple's real sandbox catalog before the Pro workflow is testable.

Send useful feedback

In Stillroom choose Help → Share Feedback. Describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. Review the complete report before saving. Optional technical details contain the app/build version, macOS, architecture, edition and current batch count. Images, filenames, paths, purchase IDs and export history are not added. Avoid private client details in your own text.

Save the report, then share it with your study organizer. You can also send feedback using TestFlight; review screenshots before sending them because they may include filenames or client work. Stillroom does not send its saved report automatically.

For an incorrect output, describe the format, approximate size, crop and settings. Only provide an image if you choose to create a non-confidential reproduction. Include whether the issue repeats and whether retrying helped.

Known limitations

  • English interface; uncompleted VoiceOver and older-OS/Intel hardware validation.
  • Free export: three sources per batch. Pro enables larger batches, limits and delivery sets.
  • At most 300 inputs, 250 MB/120 megapixels per input, 32 megapixels per output. A strict byte limit may reduce quality and dimensions.
  • Animation and multipage documents are rejected. PNG preserves transparency; JPEG fills transparent areas white. Outputs use sRGB; HDR/wide-gamut handling needs further study.
  • Suggestions use on-device saliency and can choose the wrong subject. Always review before Apply.
  • Zoom is relative to Fit. The reference/export wipe compares matching crop and output dimensions, not the untouched full-resolution source.
  • Cancelling keeps completed files. A cancelled delivery may have a manifest without a contact sheet. Source files remain unchanged.

Apple's TestFlight information: https://testflight.apple.com/

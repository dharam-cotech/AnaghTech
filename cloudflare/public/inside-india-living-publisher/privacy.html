Inside India Living Publisher

Privacy policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Inside India Living Publisher is a desktop publishing tool operated by Anagh Tech for the Inside India Living YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. This policy explains how the tool handles data obtained through Google authorization. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google data the publisher accesses

With the channel operator's consent, the tool requests YouTube read-only and video upload permissions. It reads the connected channel's ID and name to confirm the correct destination. It reads video IDs, upload and processing status, visibility, and other video details needed to confirm or reconcile a publication. It uploads prepared video files, thumbnails and their publication metadata to YouTube. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts, or Google Drive.

How the data is used

The tool uses this information to verify the destination channel, publish videos selected by the operator, check whether uploads completed, and record the outcome for scheduling and troubleshooting. It does not use Google data for advertising or sell it.

Storage and security

Google OAuth credentials are kept in encrypted local storage on the operator's computer. The encryption key is held in macOS Keychain. Local publication records can contain channel and video identifiers, video links, metadata, status, and diagnostic evidence. Access to the computer and its backups is managed by the operator. OAuth credentials are not placed on this website.

Sharing

The tool sends the selected video and publication details to YouTube to perform the upload. After a successful publication, the operator's configured Slack workspace may receive a notification containing the video title, YouTube link and visibility. This can include a link to a private video, so access to that Slack channel should be limited to the intended recipients. Google OAuth tokens are not included in Slack notifications. The tool does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers.

Retention and control

Credentials and publication records are stored on the operator's computer for publishing and troubleshooting until the operator removes them. The operator can revoke the tool's Google access at any time from Google security settings. Revoking access stops future authorized requests; it does not erase videos already uploaded to YouTube. To request deletion of locally held YouTube data and credentials, email the address below. Removing local records does not remove videos from YouTube; those must be managed in YouTube Studio.

This website and support email

These pages have no sign-in form, analytics scripts, or advertising cookies added by Anagh Tech. Cloudflare hosts the website and may process normal request data such as IP addresses under its privacy policy. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send, and use it to respond to your request.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or data requests, contact anagh.techn@gmail.com.

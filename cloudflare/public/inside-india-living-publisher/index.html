Video publishing

Inside India Living Publisher

A desktop tool used by the channel operator to publish prepared videos to the Inside India Living YouTube channel.

The publisher connects to YouTube with the operator's permission. Before an upload, it checks that the connected account controls the intended channel. It uploads the prepared video and its title, description, category and other publication settings, then checks the resulting video status. The operator can review the published video in YouTube Studio.

How the connection is used

Google authorization is used for channel verification and YouTube video publishing. The tool runs for the channel operator; this website provides information about the tool and does not accept uploads or Google sign-in.

Read the privacy policy for the Google data accessed, where it is stored, and how access can be revoked. The terms of service explain the tool's permitted use.

Inside India Living Publisher

Terms of service

Effective September 25, 2026

Inside India Living Publisher is a desktop tool operated by Anagh Tech to publish prepared videos to the Inside India Living YouTube channel. It is intended for the channel operator and people the operator authorizes.

Using the publisher

Use the tool only with a YouTube channel you are authorized to manage and only for videos you have the right to publish. You are responsible for reviewing the video, metadata, visibility, and schedule before publication. By using the publisher, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google connection

The tool requires Google authorization to confirm the channel, upload videos, and verify results. You may revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized operations until the tool is connected again. See the privacy policy for details about data handling.

Availability and contact

Uploads depend on Google and YouTube services, the operator's connection, and account permissions. The operator should check YouTube Studio for the final state of a video. For questions about this tool, contact anagh.techn@gmail.com.

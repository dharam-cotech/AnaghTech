Privacy & data handling

Your images stay
on your Mac.

Stillroom processes images locally. The app has no developer analytics SDK, advertisements, image-upload service or account system.

Files and local settings

The app reads images you choose and writes new files to a destination you choose. It stores recipes, delivery sets, last-used settings and up to 100 export records locally. Records contain dates, counts, byte sizes, destination folders and security-scoped bookmarks. They do not contain image copies. Clear history in Settings; exported files and recipes are kept.

The source queue and per-image crop positions last for the current session. Optional crop suggestions run on your Mac using Apple Vision. Exported images are rendered in sRGB without original location, camera or capture-date metadata. Delivery manifests and contact sheets include source filenames and output specifications; check them before sharing.

Apple purchases

Apple handles payments and subscription validation. Stillroom contacts the App Store through StoreKit and receives product and verified entitlement information; it does not receive payment-card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those services.

Voluntary feedback

Help → Share Feedback creates a text report locally. You can exclude technical details and review the complete report before saving. The report contains your typed feedback and, if selected, app/build version, macOS version, architecture, edition and batch count. Stillroom adds no images, filenames, folder paths, purchase IDs or export history. It does not automatically send the file.

If you choose to send feedback, its recipient receives the content you share. Avoid confidential information in text or screenshots. If you email Anagh Tech, we receive your email address, message and attachments. We use them to respond and investigate issues, retaining correspondence as needed for those purposes. Contact us to request deletion. Participation in an observed beta study is optional and requires separate consent.

TestFlight

Apple's TestFlight service separately collects and shares information with developers, including crash logs, usage information and feedback. Review Apple's TestFlight information before joining. Test purchases during the beta are not real payments.

This website

These pages include no analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. The hosting service may process network requests, including IP addresses. This website is served through Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Anagh Tech adds no analytics or advertising cookies.

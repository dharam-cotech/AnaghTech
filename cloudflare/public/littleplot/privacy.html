Little Plot, Big Plans · Version 1.0

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Little Plot stores your garden progress on your device. There is no app account, advertising, or cross-app tracking. Optional anonymous analytics and crash reporting are off until you enable them in Settings. Apple handles in-app purchases. You choose whether to contact support or share a garden.

Information stored on your device

The app stores completed garden IDs, unfinished puzzle states and move history, saved level definitions, music/sound/haptic preferences, finished journal plantings, and whether you completed practice. New journal plantings can include their save date. This information is not automatically sent to an Anagh Tech server.

The app does not provide cloud sync in this version. Local app files may be included in device backups according to your Apple and device settings. Deleting the app removes its local data subject to iOS behavior; separate device backups are managed through Apple’s settings.

Purchases

The optional full-game unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. The app checks Apple-verified purchase information to determine access and supports restoring ownership. Anagh Tech does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple’s services are subject to Apple’s privacy policy. Restoring ownership does not restore local puzzle progress.

Sharing and support

Sharing a garden creates an image on your device and opens the system share interface. You choose the recipient or destination; the app does not upload it automatically.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to attach. The feedback draft includes the app version/build but does not automatically attach progress files. We use correspondence to respond and investigate issues, and retain it as needed for those purposes. To ask about or request deletion of support correspondence, email anagh.techn@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or a child’s personal information.

Network access and external links

Core puzzle play, practice, and the journal work offline. If you enable analytics and crash reporting, the app communicates with PostHog. App Store checks, purchases, and restoration communicate with Apple. Support, privacy, App Store, email, and sharing links open their respective services. Those services may process information under their own policies.

This website is served through Cloudflare. Visiting it involves normal web requests to Cloudflare, which may process technical information such as IP addresses under Cloudflare’s privacy policy. These pages contain no analytics scripts or advertising cookies added by Anagh Tech.

Optional analytics and crash reports

If you turn on Share analytics & crash reports in Settings, Little Plot uses PostHog to receive an app-generated anonymous identifier, app version/build, device and operating-system diagnostics, bounded feature-use events, and technical crash reports. Feature-use events can include the selected tab, garden number, whether a garden is free, move count, completion state, hint or Undo use, and purchase-flow result categories. Crash reports can include stack traces and short technical breadcrumbs containing those bounded values.

The app does not send planted-cell coordinates, board layouts, journal pictures, support messages, names, email addresses, Apple Account information, payment details, or purchase transaction identifiers to PostHog. Session replay, screen and tap autocapture, surveys, advertising profiles, and precise geolocation are disabled. This information is used only to understand feature use, diagnose failures, and improve Little Plot. It is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops new app analytics and closes the PostHog client; information already received is not automatically deleted. PostHog processes this data as our service provider under PostHog’s privacy policy. Apple’s operating-system diagnostics may also be available according to your device settings.

Children and families

The game does not request names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, or an online account to play. A parent or guardian should supervise purchases and external sharing. Support requests should avoid including children’s personal information.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its date, and update the associated App Store disclosures where applicable.

For privacy questions or support, contact anagh.techn@gmail.com.

Planting a garden

How do I plant?

Choose a seed packet, then touch empty brown soil to preview the planting. Release to plant, or slide away to cancel. Each valid planting uses one move. Learn to plant in Home or Settings offers an optional practice bed that does not change your campaign progress.

How does each crop grow?

Carrots grow right, cauliflower grows up, and beetroot grows diagonally up-right. The first plant or missing soil stops these lines. Pumpkins grow at the selected square and its four neighboring squares, using only available soil.

Why can a garden finish with empty squares?

You win by reaching every crop goal within the move allowance. You do not need to fill all the soil. Extra crops are allowed, but they use space. Green patches are not planting soil.

Why can’t I add another crop?

Check the remaining moves. Once you have used them all, you cannot plant again even if soil is empty. Choose Undo to restore a move, use a hint, or restart this planting from the garden menu.

Do hints, previews, or mistakes use moves?

No. Changing seeds, previewing, cancelling a preview, asking for a hint, and invalid taps do not spend moves. Undo restores the previous planting and its move. Restart clears the current planting and restores its full move allowance.

Your collection and purchase

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

The first five gardens are free. One non-consumable purchase unlocks access to gardens 6–50. The US price is $2.99; the App Store displays your region’s price before purchase. There is no subscription and no advertising. Complete each garden to reach the next. Practice and the journal are available to free players too.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Internet access is needed to contact the App Store. Purchase restoration restores full-game access; it does not restore your garden progress.

Are my gardens saved across devices?

This version saves garden progress, unfinished plantings, preferences, and the journal on this device. It does not provide in-app cloud sync or an online account. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. Deleting the app can remove local progress; restoring the purchase alone will not bring that progress back.

Why is an earlier journal picture unavailable?

Your completion is still credited. An older version or an unfinished replay may not have retained the finished planting. Grow that garden again to add a picture. The journal keeps the latest successfully saved planting even if you later Undo or start a new attempt.

Does the game work offline?

Puzzle play, practice, and the local journal work offline. Buying or restoring access uses Apple’s services. Opening support, privacy, email, and sharing destinations may use the internet.

Where are sound, haptics, and accessibility options?

Music, garden sounds, and haptics can be adjusted in Settings. The app follows supported iOS text-size and Reduce Motion settings. The planting guide and practice include text explanations and accessible controls.

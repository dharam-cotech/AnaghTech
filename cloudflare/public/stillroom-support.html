Stillroom support

A little help,
when you need it.

Prepare images on your Mac, check the result, and deliver new files while keeping your originals.

Start with one image

  1. Choose Try a sample, or add an image with ⌘O.
  2. Choose a recipe. Inspect the encoded preview, compare it with the matching reference, and adjust the crop if needed.
  3. Press ⌘E and choose an output folder. Stillroom adds a number when a filename already exists.

Working with delivery sets

Delivery sets prepare several versions of each source and organize them into folders. A completed delivery includes a PDF contact sheet and a CSV manifest based on the files actually written. This workflow requires Pro access. Inspect each variant: crop position is shared per source and size targets can reduce quality and dimensions.

Something did not work?

Choose Help → Share Feedback in the app. Describe your task, what happened and what you expected. Review the report before saving. You choose whether to share it; Stillroom does not send it automatically.

Leave out confidential client details. Images, filenames, folder paths, purchase IDs and export history are not added to the report. Optional diagnostics show only the app version, macOS, architecture, edition and batch count.

Contact anagh.techn@gmail.com. Include your app version and a short description; do not send confidential images.

Common questions

Where are my originals?

Stillroom reads them in place and creates new output files. Closing the app clears the working queue; it does not delete your images. Recipes, sets, last-used settings and export history stay on this Mac.

Why is Pro unavailable?

The App Store catalog and a verified subscription must be available. Try Check availability or Restore Purchases in the Pro sheet. TestFlight purchases are free sandbox tests and do not carry over to an App Store release.

Why did the dimensions change?

Stillroom never enlarges a smaller original. A file-size limit may reduce dimensions further. Check the actual dimensions and bytes in the preview and output manifest.

What happens when I cancel?

Completed files are kept. A cancelled delivery may include a manifest without a contact sheet. You can start a new export after cancellation finishes.

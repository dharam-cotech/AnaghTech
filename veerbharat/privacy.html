Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Overview

Veer Bharat ("Warriors of Bharat") is an educational iPhone and iPad app designed for young learners and families. This policy explains how Version 1.3 handles information.

Data Collection

Veer Bharat does not require an account. The Version 1.3 app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect personal information through the app.

What Data Is Stored

The app stores learning preferences and progress locally on the device. This can include the selected reading level, points, streaks, completed quizzes and stories, best quiz scores, earned rewards, and unlocked cards. This data:

  • Is stored locally using standard iOS storage
  • Is not sent to a developer-operated server
  • Can be removed by resetting progress in Parent Zone or deleting the app
  • May be included in device backups according to the user's Apple and device settings

Internet & Network

The core learning experience does not require an internet connection. Parent Zone contains links to this policy and the support page. Opening one of these links leaves the app and uses the device's browser; the browser and hosting provider may process information under their own policies.

Third-Party Services

The Version 1.3 app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. There are no ads in the app.

Optional Imaginative Story Generation

On supported devices, Veer Bharat can use Apple's on-device system language model through the Foundation Models framework to generate an imaginative story. The current implementation does not send story prompts or output to a developer-operated server.

Generated stories may be inaccurate and are presented as imaginative content, not historical sources. This feature is optional, and the core app remains usable without it.

Children's Privacy

Veer Bharat is designed to avoid requesting personal information from children. Parents and children should not send a child's personal information in support email. If you believe personal information was sent to us, please contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated. The date above identifies the latest version.

Veer Bharat

Learn About India's Greatest Heroes

Veer Bharat is an English-language educational app for iPhone and iPad. Young learners can discover India's historical figures, epic and sacred characters, and traditional accounts through profiles, quizzes, interactive stories, and collectible cards.

Features

  • 44 illustrated profiles with clear content classifications
  • 249 quiz questions with helpful learning explanations
  • 37 interactive stories with 148 chapters and meaningful choices
  • Card collection and mastery with replay-safe progress and rewards
  • Read-aloud support for key learning content
  • Family-friendly core with no account, no third-party ads, and offline access
  • Optional imaginative stories generated on supported Apple Intelligence devices

The core app supports iOS 18 and later. Optional imaginative story generation requires a compatible Apple Intelligence device and iOS 26 or later.

FAQ

  • Is the app free? — Yes, Veer Bharat is completely free.
  • Does the core app require internet? — No. Profiles, quizzes, classic stories, progress, and cards work offline.
  • What age group is it for? — Designed for kids aged 5-10.
  • Does it contain ads? — No, the app is ad-free.
  • Does it require an account? — No account or personal profile is required.
  • Are AI stories historical sources? — No. They are optional imaginative adventures and are labelled separately from reviewed local learning takeaways.
  • How can a parent change settings? — Open Parent Zone from the Home screen and complete the parent check.

Please do not include a child's name, photo, location, or other personal information in support messages.
